AI fluency in the classroom

Since May 2026 I have taught AI fluency using the 4Ds framework from my Anthropic Academy coursework, and I created a student worksheet that turns each D into a task. Students learn to direct AI tools, check the output, and keep their own judgment.

DelegationDecide which tasks to hand to AI and which to keep.
DescriptionState the task, context, and standard clearly.
DiscernmentEvaluate the output for accuracy and fit.
Due DiligenceVerify sources and own the final work.

In a business setting, each D has a concrete form. Students delegate data cleaning, describe model assumptions in writing, compare AI output against source data to find errors, and document what they verified. The work ties AI use to business ethics and critical thinking. I measure the initiative with anonymous student reflections and structured assignments. Results show higher student confidence in using AI and a clearer understanding of what it can and cannot do. I hold Anthropic Academy certificates in AI Fluency Framework and Foundations and AI Fluency for Educators.
